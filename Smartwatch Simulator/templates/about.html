About SmartGuard

Smartwatch Simulator is an AI-powered smartwatch monitoring system designed to detect risk conditions in real time. It analyzes heart rate, stress level, and health metrics to predict danger situations and trigger alerts automatically for safety.

Features

  • ✔ Real-time health monitoring
  • ✔ AI danger prediction
  • ✔ Emergency SMS alert
  • ✔ Live dashboard visualization
  • ✔ Instant risk detection
